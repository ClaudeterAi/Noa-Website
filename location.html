— The Crossing

From the
Marina to the Sea

NOA is anchored approximately two kilometres off the coast, beyond the Marina breakwater. The only way on board is by our shuttle boat from Dubai Marina Pier, Berth 7. The crossing takes 11 minutes.

Shuttles run every 30 minutes from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. The first shuttle of the day is the Morning Package check-in. The last shuttle of the night is the Afternoon Package departure. Reservations are tied to a wristband; the boat crew check at boarding.

The pier itself is reached by car, taxi or metro. Valet parking is available adjacent to the pier from 9:00 AM. From Dubai Marina metro station, the pier is a six-minute walk. A NOA-branded check-in lounge is on the pier with cold drinks and a wristband counter.

— The Arrival

A Marina
Lounge First

Check-in is at the pier lounge, not on the boat. On arrival, ID is matched to the reservation, the wristband is loaded with your F and B credit, and you are walked to the next shuttle.

We recommend arriving 30 minutes before your intended shuttle. The lounge has cold towels and a small bar — coffee in the morning, sparkling in the afternoon.
