Package 01 · Chill Vibe

Morning Package

9:30 AM — 2:00 PM. Pool, sun, daybeds and a slow first half of the day on the Gulf.
— The Session

A Slow Start
To the Day

The morning at NOA is for guests who want the water before the city wakes. We open the doors at 9:30 AM, the kitchen at the same hour, and the pool is clear, the music is low, and the deck is calm.

This is the session for breakfast on the bow, a long swim, an afternoon nap on a daybed, and a Burj Al Arab silhouette at midday. The DJ booth stays quiet until two; the energy is closer to a private members club than a beach party.

The package includes the shuttle boat from Dubai Marina Pier, a welcome drink at check-in, all-day pool access, a towel, and an F&B credit loaded onto a wristband to spend across the four bars and the restaurant.

— 01

The Infinity
Pool

Twenty-two metres of saltwater suspended above the Arabian Gulf — the longest infinity pool on water in Dubai. Sun shade umbrellas are available at every daybed. The pool is open from the first shuttle to the last.

Lifeguards are on the deck through every session. Children aged 4 and up are welcome at the morning session under the supervision of a guardian.

— 02

The Breakfast
Service

The kitchen serves a focused breakfast and lunch menu through the morning session. Cold counter, pastry station, eggs cooked to order, and a small section of mid-day plates — burrata, hand-rolled pasta, wood-fired flatbreads.

F&B credit on your wristband can be spent at the restaurant or any of the four bars on board.

Good to Know
What time should I arrive?
+

Last check-in is at 12:00 noon. We recommend arriving at the marina pier 30 minutes before your intended shuttle to allow for check-in and the crossing. Shuttles run every 30 minutes from 9:00 AM.

Can I bring children?
+

Yes — children aged 4 and above are welcome at the Morning Package. Child pricing matches adult pricing as capacity is limited. Under 4 are not permitted on board for safety reasons.

What if I want to stay for the Afternoon Package?
+

Combined morning + afternoon access is available at a reduced rate. Mention this at booking, or upgrade your wristband at check-in subject to afternoon availability.

Dress code?
+

Smart beach casual. Swimwear is welcome on the pool deck; cover-ups expected in the restaurant. No sportswear, jerseys, or flip-flops in the restaurant.
