Booking and Entry
Do I need to book in advance?
+

Walk-ins are welcome, but advance booking is strongly recommended. NOA has limited daily capacity and reserved daybeds sell out quickly, especially on weekends and event nights.

What is the cancellation policy?
+

Cancellations more than 72 hours in advance receive a full refund minus 10% service fee. Within 72 hours: non-refundable. Cabana and special event bookings are strictly non-refundable.

What is the minimum age?
+

Morning Package: minimum age 4 years. Afternoon Package: minimum age 18 years. Alcohol is not served to guests under 21 in compliance with UAE law.

Is photography allowed on board?
+

Personal photography is welcome on the open deck. Professional cameras, lenses over 200mm, drones, and any commercial filming require prior written permission. Photography of other guests requires their consent.

Do you sell gift vouchers?
+

Yes. Gift vouchers are available in any AED value at the package or cabana level. Vouchers are valid for 12 months from issue and can be redeemed for any session subject to availability.

Arrival and Access
How do I get to NOA?
+

NOA is reached by our complimentary shuttle boat from Dubai Marina Pier, Berth 7. The crossing takes 11 minutes. Shuttles run every 30 minutes from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Is parking available at the pier?
+

Yes — valet parking is available adjacent to Berth 7 from 9:00 AM. Self-park is available at the Dubai Marina public garage, a four-minute walk away. Both are paid; valet is charged to your wristband if you wish.

Can I arrive by private boat?
+

Yes. Direct anchoring alongside NOA is not permitted — our crew will guide your final approach by tender. Contact us in advance to arrange private arrival logistics from Dubai Marina.

Is the deck accessible for guests with reduced mobility?
+

The main deck, pool deck, restaurant and one accessible cabana are step-free and reachable by ramped boarding from the shuttle. The upper deck involves a short flight of stairs. Please let us know at booking so we can assign accessible seating.

On Board
What is the dress code?
+

Smart beach casual is required. We welcome linen shirts, tailored shorts, resort wear and chic cover-ups. Tank tops, ripped clothing, sports jerseys, and flip-flops are not permitted in the restaurant. NOA reserves the right to deny entry.

What's included in my package?
+

All packages include shuttle boat transfer, welcome drink, pool access, towel, and guest insurance. F and B credit is loaded to a wristband at check-in. Unused credit is non-refundable and cannot be carried over.

What happens in bad weather?
+

NOA operates in normal Gulf weather year-round. In the rare event of a sustained sandstorm or high sea state, the day will be cancelled and bookings refunded or transferred at the guest's choice. Light rain does not affect operations.

Can I bring my own food or drink?
+

No. All food and drink is supplied on board. We have a full restaurant, four bars, a cellar list, and a non-alcoholic menu including specialty coffee. Outside food and drink are not permitted, including for dietary or religious reasons — please let us know at booking and we will accommodate.

Private Events
Can NOA be hired for a private event?
+

Yes — full venue hire is available on selected days, and partial hire (the upper deck, the restaurant, a cabana cluster) is available throughout the week. Capacity for full hire is 280. Contact our private events team for a proposal.

What's the minimum spend for a group booking?
+

Groups of 12 or more receive a custom proposal with a minimum spend tied to the day, the session and the deck configuration. Cabana clusters and private decks are available for groups of 12 to 48.
