Package 02 · Party Vibe

Afternoon Package

12:00 PM to 9:30 PM. Full pool, the DJ booth on, and Dubai's longest sunset.
— The Session

The Pool, the
Sound, the Sun

The afternoon session is NOA at full tilt. The pool fills, the DJ booth comes on at 2:00 PM, the kitchen pivots to its lunch and dinner menus, and the energy rises through the afternoon into a long, golden Dubai sunset.

By six the music is in full flow and the sky behind the Burj Al Arab is going pink. By nine the bar has emptied its first run of cocktails. By half past, the last shuttle pulls away from the pier and the deck goes dark for the night.

The package includes the shuttle boat from Dubai Marina Pier, a welcome drink at check-in, full pool access, a towel, and an F and B credit loaded onto a wristband. The afternoon session is 18+.

— 01

The DJ
Programme

A resident DJ takes the booth at 2 PM every day. On Fridays and Saturdays, a guest headliner joins — names you will recognise from Ibiza, Berlin and Lisbon. The full programme is published at the start of every month.

The booth sits at the bow of the upper deck, facing the city. The sound is calibrated for the open water — close on the deck, soft over the pool below.

— 02

The Late
Kitchen

The kitchen serves a fuller dinner menu from 5 PM. Larger sharing plates, the wood-fired grill at full output, signature whole-fish bakes, and a small but considered list of desserts.

F and B credit on your wristband applies anywhere on board, including the restaurant and all four bars. The kitchen closes at 9:00 PM; the bars close with the session.

Good to Know
What time should I arrive?
+

Last check-in is at 7:00 PM, but most guests arrive between 2 and 5 PM when the DJ starts and the sun is high. Shuttles run every 30 minutes from 11:30 AM.

Is the afternoon session 18+?
+

Yes. The afternoon session is 18+ only. ID is checked at the marina pier check-in. Guests under 18 are welcome at the Morning Package.

Dress code for the afternoon?
+

Elevated smart beach casual. Swimwear on the pool deck is fine; cover-ups expected in the restaurant and on the upper deck. No sportswear, no flip-flops on the restaurant floor.

Can I book a table at the restaurant?
+

Yes — restaurant reservations are taken separately at booking. Tables held for 90 minutes. Walk-ins are seated subject to availability.
