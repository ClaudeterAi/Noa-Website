— About

A Beach Club on the Sea

Dubai's first floating beach club. Anchored in the Arabian Gulf. Opening 2026.
— The Idea

Why a Floating
Beach Club

The idea behind NOA was simple. Dubai already had every kind of beach club on the sand. What it didn't have was a club on the water itself — open Gulf in every direction, the city skyline in the distance, no walls, no neighbours, no kerb to step back onto.

NOA is anchored two kilometres off Dubai Marina. You arrive by shuttle boat, you spend the day on a 280-capacity deck built around a 22-metre infinity pool, and at the end of the day you leave by the same shuttle. There is no road in. There is no walk-up. The water is the venue.

We open in 2026. The deck is now in final fit-out; the bars are being staffed; the kitchen is being assembled. The opening series of nights — Claptone, Peggy Gou, Black Coffee — is announced for March. The rest follows.

— The Deck

Built for the
Open Gulf

The platform sits on a moored barge purpose-built for the Gulf — naval-architect-led, twin-anchored, motion-stabilised. The pool is saltwater, glass-sided on the outer edge, and engineered to read as level even in moderate swell.

Above the pool deck, an upper deck holds the DJ booth, the rooftop bar, and the cabana row. Below, the restaurant, the central bar, and the service galley.

— The Philosophy

A Quieter Kind
of Beach Club

NOA was designed against the trend. No Lamborghini drop-off. No bottle parade. No champagne shower over the deck. The music is calibrated, not pushed; the menu is short, not exhaustive; the lighting is candle-warm at night, not strobe.

The guest list is capped at 280 daily so the pool stays usable and the service stays close. Cabanas are sold one-per-group, not split. The room is meant to feel like a small island, not a stadium.
