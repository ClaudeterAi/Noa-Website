Package 03 · All Day · Exclusive

Private Cabana

9:30 AM to 9:30 PM. A poolside suite, a dedicated butler, and the Gulf to yourselves.
— The Reservation

Your Own Corner
of the Deck

Ten cabanas sit along the upper pool edge — each enclosed on three sides, open to the water, and yours for the full day. The cabana spans both the morning and the afternoon sessions, so the seat you sit down in at 9:30 AM is the seat you stand up from at 9:30 PM.

A butler is assigned to your cabana on arrival and stays with you for the duration. They will handle every drinks order at the bar, every plate from the restaurant, and every small request — from a phone charger to a particular bottle from the cellar.

The cabana is priced from AED 8,500 for up to six guests, including an unlimited F and B credit at all four bars and the restaurant. Premium spirits and champagne service are available on a separate menu.

— The Setup

Inside the
Cabana

A six-seat lounge on the pool side, a small private bar, a refrigerated drinks unit, side tables, and parasol-shaded daybeds spilling out to the pool edge. The view runs directly along the 22-metre pool to the Gulf horizon.

Sound is on the cabana's own discreet system, so your conversation stays your own. The DJ feed can be brought into the cabana on request.

Good to Know
What's the maximum group size in a cabana?
+

Six guests per cabana. For larger groups, cabanas can be booked side-by-side or combined into a private section of the upper deck — contact our private events team for arrangements.

What's included beyond F and B?
+

The shuttle transfer (priority slot), welcome platter on arrival, the butler service, and access to both the Morning and Afternoon sessions on the same wristband. Premium spirits and champagne are charged separately at cellar pricing.

Can the cabana be booked for just the morning or just the afternoon?
+

Cabanas are sold as all-day reservations. For half-day private use, the Front-Row Daybed in either session is the next tier down.
